Memsum handles screenshot auto-detection → notification → organizing in one flow. When you take a new screenshot, Memsum detects it and asks in a notification whether to save it. Tap [Save] and it reads the text and adds a title, summary, category and any dates, without opening the app.

Receipts, plans in a chat, coupons, articles you meant to read. If your screenshots pile up and you never find them again, Memsum pulls out what matters, like running a highlighter over the page.

[Screenshot detection and notifications]
- Even while you are using other apps, a new screenshot triggers a notification.
- [Save] organizes it in the background. [Dismiss] just clears the notification.
- Regular photos are left alone. Memsum only looks for screenshots.
- Older screenshots can be picked from your gallery and imported.

[Text recognition and auto-organizing]
- Text in the image is recognized on your device, then cleaned up for typos and line breaks.
- Each capture gets a one-line title and a one-line summary.
- Captures are sorted into six groups: marketing, events, receipts, shopping, info and other.
- Search finds words inside your screenshots, not just titles.

[Dates go to Google Calendar]
- Memsum spots dates and times in a capture.
- Connect Google Calendar to add events with one tap, or turn on auto-add in Settings.
- Calendar access is used only to add events. Memsum does not read your existing events.

[Sunday 5-line report]
- Every Sunday at 7 PM, Memsum picks five captures from the week worth a second look.
- You can turn the report notification off in Settings.

[Privacy]
- No sign-up or email needed. Start right away.
- Photo access is used only to detect and organize screenshots.
- You can delete all your data anytime from Settings.
- No ads.
