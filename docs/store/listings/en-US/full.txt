Plans you screenshot are easy to miss if you never look at them again. Memsum remembers your screenshots for you and brings them back before they slip by.

When you take a new screenshot, Memsum detects it and asks in a notification whether to save it. Tap [Save] and it reads the text and adds a title, summary, category and any dates, without opening the app. Receipts, plans in a chat, coupons, articles you meant to read: Memsum pulls out what matters, like running a highlighter over the page.

[Heads-up before you miss it]
- Home puts upcoming plans first, sorted by D-day. Today and tomorrow come first.
- The evening before, one notification lists tomorrow's plans. Pick the time in Settings.
- Every Sunday at 7 PM, Memsum picks five captures from the week worth a second look.

[Screenshot detection and notifications]
- Even while you are using other apps, a new screenshot triggers a notification.
- [Save] organizes it in the background. [Dismiss] just clears the notification.
- Regular photos are left alone. Memsum only looks for screenshots.
- Older screenshots can be picked from your gallery and imported.

[Text recognition and auto-organizing]
- Text in the image is recognized on your device, then cleaned up for typos and line breaks.
- Each capture gets a one-line title and a one-line summary.
- Captures are sorted into six groups: marketing, events, receipts, shopping, info and other.
- Search finds words inside your screenshots, not just titles.

[Dates go to Google Calendar]
- Memsum spots dates and times in a capture.
- Dates spelled out in the text are marked "Date clear"; vague ones like "next week" are marked "Check date".
- Connect Google Calendar to add events with one tap, or turn on auto-add in Settings. Auto-add only adds events with a clear date.
- Calendar access is used only to add events. Memsum does not read your existing events.

[Privacy]
- No sign-up or email needed. Start right away.
- Photo access is used only to detect and organize screenshots.
- Text is recognized on your device first; sorting and summaries run on our server. Original images are kept in private storage only you can access.
- Sensitive numbers such as card numbers on receipts are hidden on screen.
- You can delete all your data anytime from Settings.
- No ads.
